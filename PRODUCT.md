# LunaOS Control

## Product intent

LunaOS Control is Richard Gatewood's private, cross-harness control surface for
working with persistent LunaOS agents. It must make a conversation the primary
surface while keeping agent identity, memory, skills, files, and operational
tools available without forcing desktop-only workflows.

## Responsive contract

- Below 1024px, the product is chat-first: one full-width conversation is the
  active work surface.
- Sessions, files, agents, terminals, and other workspace views open as
  dismissible full-screen drawers. They never consume a persistent side rail
  or squeeze the conversation.
- At 1024px and above, the desktop layout may expose resizable side rails and
  inline workspace views.
- No normal mobile or tablet flow may create horizontal page scrolling.

## Quality bar

The phone experience must preserve the same capabilities as desktop through
clear, touch-sized controls and drawers. Attachment failures must tell the user
the real reason; a trusted private LunaOS Control origin must support live
updates and multipart uploads.
